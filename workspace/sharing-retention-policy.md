## 1. Where files live
All active business records, client quotes, and invoices for Savannah Craft Supply live in Google Shared Drives organized by department. Staff must not store company documents only in personal My Drive folders.

## 2. Sharing outside the business
External sharing is restricted to trusted suppliers and clients using view-only or comment-only links whenever possible. Anyone with the link (public) sharing is turned off across the organization, and staff must verify recipient email addresses before granting edit access.

## 3. Who can sign in
Only active employees with an assigned company Google Workspace account may sign in. 2-Step Verification (2SV) is mandatory for all accounts, and company data may only be accessed from approved, password-protected devices.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where
Active project files remain in Shared Drives until project completion, while signed tax and accounting records are retained in an Finance Archive Shared Drive for 7 years (to be checked with the business accountant). Deleted files remain in Drive Trash for 30 days before permanent removal.

## 5. The monthly export
On the last working day of each month, an administrator runs a Google Takeout export of core Shared Drive folders and critical records. The exported archive is verified by test-restoring one sample file and stored on an encrypted offline backup drive.

## 6. Who holds admin
Only the business owner and one designated backup administrator hold Super Admin privileges, using dedicated admin accounts separate from daily email use. Regular staff members have standard user access with no administrative rights.

## 7. When someone leaves
On a departing staff member's final day, an administrator immediately resets their password, revokes active sign-in sessions and 2SV backup codes, and suspends the account. Their Drive files and ownership are transferred to their manager before the account is archived or deleted.
