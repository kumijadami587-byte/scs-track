1. What I set out to do: Automate a daily reorder check in Google Apps Script on a copy of the retail shop inventory workbook so low-stock items are written to a Reorder tab and emailed to the address in Settings!B1.
2. What I did, in order: Made a named copy of the workbook with made-up test rows, got the one-page spec initialled by a trainer, committed the raw AI draft first, read and logged every line before running, worked out expected test answers by hand, ran the tests, hand-patched five lines (label, case-insensitive in/out, <= boundary, and MailApp least-privilege), and checked all nine breaks.
3. What went wrong, and how I fixed it: The draft used strict < instead of <= on line 23 (missing items whose stock equalled the reorder level), checked case-sensitive 'In'/'Out' on lines 16-17, and requested full GmailApp access on line 30; I patched those lines by hand and verified fail -> pass in the test log.
4. How long it took, against the plan: Took about 3 hours across spec sign-off, line-by-line reading log, hand-tested edge cases, and filing—right on schedule.
5. One lesson for the next person: Always commit the raw AI draft first, test the exact boundary where stock equals the reorder level, and replace GmailApp with MailApp whenever a script only sends notifications.
6. What I might be wrong about, and how I would find out: I might be wrong that item names in the owner's real sheet have no trailing spaces or typos that prevent matching between Lists and Records; I would find out by checking the Reorder output alongside the owner on their real sheet during handover.
